How LinkedIn Phone Number Scraping Works

Technical architecture, privacy mechanics, and data enrichment

1. Where are Phone Numbers on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn keeps user phone numbers strictly private behind three tiers of visibility:

  • Only Me: The user entered their phone for 2FA, but it is invisible to anyone on the web.
  • 1st-Degree Connections Only: Visible only if you send a connection request and they accept it.
  • Public Summary / Headline: When job seekers explicitly add their phone numbers or email in their public bio (e.g. "Contact: +91 98765 43210").

2. How Our Scraper Extracts Contacts Safely:
